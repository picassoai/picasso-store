AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 vs AK10-9 V2.0 KV60

The AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 has more peak torque (53 N·m against 48 N·m), and is lighter (940 g against 960 g), and turns faster at the output (235 RPM against 109/228 RPM). The AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 has a higher torque constant (0.198 N·m/A against 0.16 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (10.6 A against 10.7 A). They match on continuous torque, diameter and depth.

The AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 is US$895 and the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 is US$895. That is US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 and US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 for more peak torque, less weight and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 for more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
