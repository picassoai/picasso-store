RO40 KV140 Lite vs RO50 KV108 Lite

The RO40 KV140 Lite draws less current at its rating (2.85 A against 3.4 A), is lighter (60 g against 105 g), narrower (46.3 mm against 61.7 mm) and shallower (13 mm against 14.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (2450 RPM against 2100 RPM). The RO50 KV108 Lite has more continuous torque (0.3 N·m against 0.2 N·m), more peak torque (0.9 N·m against 0.6 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.088 N·m/A against 0.068 N·m/A).

The RO40 KV140 Lite is US$58 and the RO50 KV108 Lite is US$74. That is US$290.00 per rated N·m for the RO40 KV140 Lite and US$246.67 per rated N·m for the RO50 KV108 Lite.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO40 KV140 Lite for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RO50 KV108 Lite for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
