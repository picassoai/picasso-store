DW25 Thruster CCW

Underwater. 48 V.

US$672.00

The largest DW thruster: ≥25 kgf of bollard thrust on 1315 W at 48 V. A Ø126 mm propeller, 1790 g in air, 995 g in water and a working depth of 0–350 m. Driven by an external ESC (TW-40A-12S or TW-80A-12S), which is a separate part.

This is the counter-clockwise unit. Thrusters are specified in counter-rotating pairs so the reaction torques cancel, so a vehicle carrying more than one needs the clockwise unit for the opposing position.

Sealed underwater thruster. The working parts sit inside a sealed aluminium casing filled with thermally conductive compound, so it sheds heat into the water it is running in and keeps sand and grit out. Two-stage sealing chamber on the main shaft.
