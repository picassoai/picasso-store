AKA10-9 KV60 vs AK10-9 V2.0 KV60

The AKA10-9 KV60 has more peak torque (53 N·m against 48 N·m). The AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 has a higher torque constant (0.198 N·m/A against 0.16 N·m/A), and is lighter (960 g against 1060 g), narrower (98 mm against 100 mm) and shallower (61.7 mm against 70 mm). They match on continuous torque, current draw and output speed.

The AKA10-9 KV60 is US$895 and the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 is US$895. That is US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AKA10-9 KV60 and US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKA10-9 KV60 for more peak torque.
  • Choose the AK10-9 V2.0 KV60 for more torque per amp, less weight, a smaller diameter and less depth.
