AK45-36 KV80

AK Series. 8 N·m rated, 24 N·m peak, Ф55 mm, 340 g, 36:1 reduction, 24 V.

US$168.00

A 36:1 reduction in the 55 mm frame, which is what turns a small actuator into a load-holding one — the torque of a joint half again its diameter, at a speed to match. That trade suits a joint that spends most of its time holding a position rather than moving through one: an ankle, a wrist, a gripper that has to stay clamped. Supplied without the driver board.

Current production version: ak45-36-v3-0-kv80.
