AKE90-8 KV35 vs AK80-64 KV80

The AKE90-8 KV35 has more continuous torque (55 N·m against 48 N·m), more peak torque (170 N·m against 120 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.272 N·m/A against 0.136 N·m/A), and is shallower (43.5 mm against 61.9 mm), and turns faster at the output (120 RPM against 23/48 RPM). The AK80-64 KV80 draws less current at its rating (7 A against 21 A), and is lighter (850 g against 1400 g) and narrower (98 mm against 107.5 mm).

The AKE90-8 KV35 is US$542 and the AK80-64 KV80 is US$1109. That is US$9.85 per rated N·m for the AKE90-8 KV35 and US$23.10 per rated N·m for the AK80-64 KV80.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKE90-8 KV35 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK80-64 KV80 for a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
