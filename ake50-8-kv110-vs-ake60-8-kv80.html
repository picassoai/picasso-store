AKE50-8 KV110 vs AKE60-8 KV80

The AKE50-8 KV110 draws less current at its rating (3 A against 4.8 A), is lighter (190 g against 260 g), narrower (61.2 mm against 69 mm) and shallower (22.5 mm against 25 mm), and turns faster at the output (240 RPM against 180 RPM). The AKE60-8 KV80 has more continuous torque (5 N·m against 2.5 N·m), more peak torque (12.5 N·m against 7.5 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.13 N·m/A against 0.104 N·m/A).

The AKE50-8 KV110 is US$190 and the AKE60-8 KV80 is US$245. That is US$76.00 per rated N·m for the AKE50-8 KV110 and US$49.00 per rated N·m for the AKE60-8 KV80.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKE50-8 KV110 for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AKE60-8 KV80 for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
