GL60 Ⅱ KV28 vs GL80 KV30

The GL60 Ⅱ KV28 draws less current at its rating (1.56 A against 2.8 A), and is lighter (276 g against 315 g) and narrower (70.5 mm against 87 mm). The GL80 KV30 has more continuous torque (1 N·m against 0.6 N·m) and more peak torque (2.9 N·m against 1 N·m), is shallower (22.3 mm against 33.6 mm), and turns faster at the output (450 RPM against 153 RPM).

The GL60 Ⅱ KV28 is US$187 and the GL80 KV30 is US$204. That is US$311.67 per rated N·m for the GL60 Ⅱ KV28 and US$204.00 per rated N·m for the GL80 KV30.

Which to buy

  • Choose the GL60 Ⅱ KV28 for a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
  • Choose the GL80 KV30 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, less depth and more output speed.
