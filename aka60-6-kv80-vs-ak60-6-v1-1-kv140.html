AKA60-6 KV80 vs AK60-6 V1.1 KV140

The AKA60-6 KV80 has a higher torque constant (0.11937 N·m/A against 0.078 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (4 A against 6.5 A). The AK60-6 V1.1 KV140 is lighter (368 g against 460 g), narrower (79 mm against 80 mm) and shallower (39.5 mm against 51.2 mm), and turns faster at the output (420 RPM against 200/400 RPM). They match on continuous torque and peak torque.

The AKA60-6 KV80 is US$335 and the AK60-6 V1.1 KV140 is US$335. That is US$111.67 per rated N·m for the AKA60-6 KV80 and US$111.67 per rated N·m for the AK60-6 V1.1 KV140.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKA60-6 KV80 for more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
  • Choose the AK60-6 V1.1 KV140 for less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
