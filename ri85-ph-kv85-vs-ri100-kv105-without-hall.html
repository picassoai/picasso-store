RI85-PH KV85 vs RI100 KV105 without hall sensor

The RI85-PH KV85 has more continuous torque (2 N·m against 1.76 N·m) and more peak torque (5 N·m against 4.95 N·m), and is lighter (411 g against 500 g) and narrower (85 mm against 104 mm), and turns faster at the output (3042 RPM against 1370/2100/2825 RPM). The RI100 KV105 without hall sensor draws less current at its rating (13.6 A against 16 A), and is shallower (26 mm against 27.3 mm).

The RI85-PH KV85 is US$166 and the RI100 KV105 without hall sensor is US$167. That is US$83.00 per rated N·m for the RI85-PH KV85 and US$94.89 per rated N·m for the RI100 KV105 without hall sensor.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RI85-PH KV85 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, less weight, a smaller diameter and more output speed.
  • Choose the RI100 KV105 without hall sensor for a lower current draw and less depth.
