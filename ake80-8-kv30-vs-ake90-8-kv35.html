AKE80-8 KV30 vs AKE90-8 KV35

The AKE80-8 KV30 has a higher torque constant (0.32 N·m/A against 0.272 N·m/A), draws less current at its rating (4.8 A against 21 A), is lighter (570 g against 1400 g), narrower (87 mm against 107.5 mm) and shallower (32 mm against 43.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (150 RPM against 120 RPM). The AKE90-8 KV35 has more continuous torque (55 N·m against 12 N·m) and more peak torque (170 N·m against 30 N·m).

The AKE80-8 KV30 is US$381 and the AKE90-8 KV35 is US$542. That is US$31.75 per rated N·m for the AKE80-8 KV30 and US$9.85 per rated N·m for the AKE90-8 KV35.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKE80-8 KV30 for more torque per amp, a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AKE90-8 KV35 for more continuous torque and more peak torque.
