RI30 KV260 vs RI60 KV120 without hall sensor

The RI30 KV260 draws less current at its rating (1.98 A against 5.6 A), is lighter (40 g against 155.9 g), narrower (38 mm against 60 mm) and shallower (14 mm against 23 mm), and turns faster at the output (4110 RPM against 1440/2320/3190 RPM). The RI60 KV120 without hall sensor has more continuous torque (0.57 N·m against 0.076 N·m), more peak torque (1.63 N·m against 0.23 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.100 N·m/A against 0.03673 N·m/A).

The RI30 KV260 is US$58 and the RI60 KV120 without hall sensor is US$111. That is US$763.16 per rated N·m for the RI30 KV260 and US$194.74 per rated N·m for the RI60 KV120 without hall sensor.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RI30 KV260 for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RI60 KV120 without hall sensor for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
