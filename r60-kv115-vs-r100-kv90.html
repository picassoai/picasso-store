R60 KV115 vs R100 KV90

The R60 KV115 draws less current at its rating (9 A against 35 A), and is lighter (248 g against 731 g) and narrower (69 mm against 106.8 mm). The R100 KV90 has more continuous torque (3.7 N·m against 0.75 N·m), more peak torque (11 N·m against 2.3 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.106 N·m/A against 0.083 N·m/A).

The R60 KV115 is US$199 and the R100 KV90 is US$478. That is US$265.33 per rated N·m for the R60 KV115 and US$129.19 per rated N·m for the R100 KV90.

Which to buy

  • Choose the R60 KV115 for a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
  • Choose the R100 KV90 for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
