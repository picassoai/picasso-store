RO100 KV55 Standard

RO Series. 4 N·m rated, 12 N·m peak, Ф113.5 mm, 710 g, 48 V.

US$193.00

The largest RO: 4 N·m rated and 12 N·m peak from a Ф113.5 x 36.2 mm, 710 g frame, on 48 V. Kv 55. CubeMars lists it for cobot arms and exoskeletons.
