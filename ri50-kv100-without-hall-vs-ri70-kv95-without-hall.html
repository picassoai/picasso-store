RI50 KV100 without hall sensor vs RI70 KV95 without hall sensor

The RI50 KV100 without hall sensor draws less current at its rating (4.8 A against 7.1 A), and is lighter (180.8 g against 270.4 g) and narrower (54 mm against 76 mm). The RI70 KV95 without hall sensor has more continuous torque (0.94 N·m against 0.58 N·m), more peak torque (2.68 N·m against 1.67 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.130 N·m/A against 0.120 N·m/A), is shallower (24 mm against 27 mm), and turns faster at the output (1270/1990/2710 RPM against 1090/1860/2600 RPM).

The RI50 KV100 without hall sensor is US$69 and the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor is US$139. That is US$118.97 per rated N·m for the RI50 KV100 without hall sensor and US$147.87 per rated N·m for the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RI50 KV100 without hall sensor for a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
  • Choose the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp, less depth and more output speed.
