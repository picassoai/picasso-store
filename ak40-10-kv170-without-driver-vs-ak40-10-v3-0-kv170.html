AK40-10 KV170 vs AK40-10 V3.0 KV170

The AK40-10 KV170 is shallower (37 mm against 40.2 mm). The AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 is lighter (190 g against 200 g). They match on continuous torque, peak torque, torque constant, current draw, diameter and output speed.
