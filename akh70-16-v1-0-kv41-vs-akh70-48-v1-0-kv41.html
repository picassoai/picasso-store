AKH70-16 V1.0 KV41 vs AKH70-48 V1.0 KV41

The AKH70-16 V1.0 KV41 is lighter (879 g against 1396 g) and shallower (60.5 mm against 81.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (90 RPM against 28 RPM). The AKH70-48 V1.0 KV41 has more continuous torque (74 N·m against 26 N·m) and more peak torque (222 N·m against 85 N·m), and draws less current at its rating (6 A against 6.5 A). They match on torque constant and diameter.

The AKH70-16 V1.0 KV41 is US$670 and the AKH70-48 V1.0 KV41 is US$782. That is US$25.77 per rated N·m for the AKH70-16 V1.0 KV41 and US$10.57 per rated N·m for the AKH70-48 V1.0 KV41.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKH70-16 V1.0 KV41 for less weight, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AKH70-48 V1.0 KV41 for more continuous torque, more peak torque and a lower current draw.
