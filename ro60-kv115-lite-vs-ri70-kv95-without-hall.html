RO60 KV115 Lite vs RI70 KV95 without hall sensor

The RO60 KV115 Lite is lighter (195 g against 270.4 g), narrower (70.5 mm against 76 mm) and shallower (15 mm against 24 mm), and turns faster at the output (4200 RPM against 1270/1990/2710 RPM). The RI70 KV95 without hall sensor has more continuous torque (0.94 N·m against 0.8 N·m), more peak torque (2.68 N·m against 2.4 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.130 N·m/A against 0.094 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (7.1 A against 8.5 A).

The RO60 KV115 Lite is US$87 and the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor is US$139. That is US$108.75 per rated N·m for the RO60 KV115 Lite and US$147.87 per rated N·m for the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO60 KV115 Lite for less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
