GL80 KV60 vs GL100 KV10

The GL80 KV60 is lighter (315 g against 698 g), narrower (87 mm against 106.8 mm) and shallower (22.3 mm against 34.2 mm), and turns faster at the output (1010 RPM against 130 RPM). The GL100 KV10 has more continuous torque (3 N·m against 1 N·m), more peak torque (7.7 N·m against 2.9 N·m) and a higher torque constant (1.030 N·m/A against 0.178 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (2.9 A against 5.6 A).

The GL80 KV60 is US$204 and the GL100 KV10 is US$306. That is US$204.00 per rated N·m for the GL80 KV60 and US$102.00 per rated N·m for the GL100 KV10.

Which to buy

  • Choose the GL80 KV60 for less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the GL100 KV10 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
