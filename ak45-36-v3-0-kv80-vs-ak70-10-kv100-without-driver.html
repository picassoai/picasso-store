AK45-36 V3.0 KV80 vs AK70-10 KV100 without driver

The AK45-36 V3.0 KV80 draws less current at its rating (2 A against 7.2 A), and is lighter (349 g against 621 g) and narrower (55 mm against 89 mm). The AK70-10 KV100 without driver has more continuous torque (8.3 N·m against 8 N·m), more peak torque (24.8 N·m against 24 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.123 N·m/A against 0.11 N·m/A), is shallower (50.25 mm against 56.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (148/310 RPM against 40 RPM).

The AK45-36 V3.0 KV80 is US$208 and the AK70-10 KV100 without driver is US$447. That is US$26.00 per rated N·m for the AK45-36 V3.0 KV80 and US$53.86 per rated N·m for the AK70-10 KV100 without driver.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK45-36 V3.0 KV80 for a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
  • Choose the AK70-10 KV100 without driver for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp, less depth and more output speed.
