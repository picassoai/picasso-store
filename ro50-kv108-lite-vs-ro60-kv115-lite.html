RO50 KV108 Lite vs RO60 KV115 Lite

The RO50 KV108 Lite draws less current at its rating (3.4 A against 8.5 A), and is lighter (105 g against 195 g), narrower (61.7 mm against 70.5 mm) and shallower (14.5 mm against 15 mm). The RO60 KV115 Lite has more continuous torque (0.8 N·m against 0.3 N·m), more peak torque (2.4 N·m against 0.9 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.094 N·m/A against 0.088 N·m/A), and turns faster at the output (4200 RPM against 2100 RPM).

The RO50 KV108 Lite is US$74 and the RO60 KV115 Lite is US$87. That is US$246.67 per rated N·m for the RO50 KV108 Lite and US$108.75 per rated N·m for the RO60 KV115 Lite.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO50 KV108 Lite for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter and less depth.
  • Choose the RO60 KV115 Lite for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp and more output speed.
