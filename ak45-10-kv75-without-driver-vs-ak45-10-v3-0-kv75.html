AK45-10 KV75 vs AK45-10 V3.0 KV75

The AK45-10 KV75 is lighter (260 g against 262 g) and shallower (43 mm against 45.2 mm), and turns faster at the output (150 RPM against 120 RPM). The AK45-10 V3.0 KV75 draws less current at its rating (1.9 A against 2.1 A). They match on continuous torque, peak torque, torque constant and diameter.

The AK45-10 KV75 is US$134 and the AK45-10 V3.0 KV75 is US$175. That is US$53.60 per rated N·m for the AK45-10 KV75 and US$70.00 per rated N·m for the AK45-10 V3.0 KV75.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK45-10 KV75 for less weight, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK45-10 V3.0 KV75 for a lower current draw.
