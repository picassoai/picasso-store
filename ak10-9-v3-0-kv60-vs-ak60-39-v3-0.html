AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 vs AK60-39 V3.0

The AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 has a higher torque constant (0.16 N·m/A against 0.12 N·m/A), and turns faster at the output (235 RPM against 70 RPM). The AK60-39 V3.0 has more continuous torque (24 N·m against 18 N·m) and more peak torque (72 N·m against 53 N·m), draws less current at its rating (4.5 A against 10.7 A), and is lighter (750 g against 940 g), narrower (79 mm against 98 mm) and shallower (48.5 mm against 61.7 mm).

The AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 is US$895 and the AK60-39 V3.0 is US$503. That is US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 and US$20.96 per rated N·m for the AK60-39 V3.0.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK10-9 V3.0 KV60 for more torque per amp and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK60-39 V3.0 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter and less depth.
