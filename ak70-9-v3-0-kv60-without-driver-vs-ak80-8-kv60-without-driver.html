AK70-9 V3.0 KV60 without driver vs AK80-8 KV60 without driver

The AK70-9 V3.0 KV60 without driver has more peak torque (29.2 N·m against 25 N·m), draws less current at its rating (6.25 A against 6.9 A), is lighter (540 g against 570 g) and narrower (89 mm against 98 mm), and turns faster at the output (260 RPM against 243 RPM). The AK80-8 KV60 without driver has more continuous torque (10 N·m against 8.5 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.199 N·m/A against 0.159 N·m/A), and is shallower (43.9 mm against 49 mm).

The AK70-9 V3.0 KV60 without driver is US$447 and the AK80-8 KV60 without driver is US$526. That is US$52.59 per rated N·m for the AK70-9 V3.0 KV60 without driver and US$52.60 per rated N·m for the AK80-8 KV60 without driver.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK70-9 V3.0 KV60 without driver for more peak torque, a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK80-8 KV60 without driver for more continuous torque, more torque per amp and less depth.
