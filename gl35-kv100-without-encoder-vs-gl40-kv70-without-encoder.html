GL35 KV100 without encoder vs GL40 KV70 without encoder

The GL35 KV100 without encoder draws less current at its rating (1.3 A against 1.62 A), is lighter (90 g against 107 g), narrower (41.8 mm against 46.5 mm) and shallower (21 mm against 21.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (815 RPM against 430 RPM). The GL40 KV70 without encoder has more continuous torque (0.25 N·m against 0.15 N·m), more peak torque (0.5 N·m against 0.46 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.150 N·m/A against 0.115 N·m/A).

The GL35 KV100 without encoder is US$62 and the GL40 KV70 without encoder is US$82. That is US$413.33 per rated N·m for the GL35 KV100 without encoder and US$328.00 per rated N·m for the GL40 KV70 without encoder.

Which to buy

  • Choose the GL35 KV100 without encoder for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
