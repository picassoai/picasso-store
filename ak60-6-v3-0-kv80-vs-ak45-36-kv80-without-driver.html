AK60-6 V3.0 KV80 vs AK45-36 KV80

The AK60-6 V3.0 KV80 has a higher torque constant (0.135 N·m/A against 0.11 N·m/A), is shallower (43 mm against 54 mm), and turns faster at the output (233/490 RPM against 40 RPM). The AK45-36 KV80 has more continuous torque (8 N·m against 3 N·m) and more peak torque (24 N·m against 9 N·m), draws less current at its rating (2 A against 3.8 A), and is lighter (340 g against 380 g) and narrower (55 mm against 79 mm).

The AK60-6 V3.0 KV80 is US$335 and the AK45-36 KV80 is US$168. That is US$111.67 per rated N·m for the AK60-6 V3.0 KV80 and US$21.00 per rated N·m for the AK45-36 KV80.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK60-6 V3.0 KV80 for more torque per amp, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK45-36 KV80 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, a lower current draw, less weight and a smaller diameter.
