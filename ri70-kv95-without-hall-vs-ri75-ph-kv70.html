RI70 KV95 without hall sensor vs RI75-PH KV70

The RI70 KV95 without hall sensor draws less current at its rating (7.1 A against 7.28 A), and is lighter (270.4 g against 348 g) and shallower (24 mm against 31.8 mm). The RI75-PH KV70 has more continuous torque (1.2 N·m against 0.94 N·m) and more peak torque (3.8 N·m against 2.68 N·m), is narrower (70 mm against 76 mm), and turns faster at the output (2365 RPM against 1270/1990/2710 RPM).

The RI70 KV95 without hall sensor is US$139 and the RI75-PH KV70 is US$156. That is US$147.87 per rated N·m for the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor and US$130.00 per rated N·m for the RI75-PH KV70.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RI70 KV95 without hall sensor for a lower current draw, less weight and less depth.
  • Choose the RI75-PH KV70 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, a smaller diameter and more output speed.
