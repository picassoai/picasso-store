AK70-10 KV100 without driver vs AK80-9 V3.0 KV100 without driver

The AK70-10 KV100 without driver has more peak torque (24.8 N·m against 22 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.123 N·m/A against 0.095 N·m/A), draws less current at its rating (7.2 A against 12 A), and is narrower (89 mm against 98 mm). The AK80-9 V3.0 KV100 without driver has more continuous torque (9 N·m against 8.3 N·m), is lighter (490 g against 621 g) and shallower (38.5 mm against 50.25 mm), and turns faster at the output (390 RPM against 148/310 RPM).

The AK70-10 KV100 without driver is US$447 and the AK80-9 V3.0 KV100 without driver is US$537. That is US$53.86 per rated N·m for the AK70-10 KV100 without driver and US$59.67 per rated N·m for the AK80-9 V3.0 KV100 without driver.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK70-10 KV100 without driver for more peak torque, more torque per amp, a lower current draw and a smaller diameter.
  • Choose the AK80-9 V3.0 KV100 without driver for more continuous torque, less weight, less depth and more output speed.
