RO80 KV105 Lite vs RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor

The RO80 KV105 Lite has more peak torque (4.5 N·m against 4.1 N·m), and is lighter (265 g against 411 g) and shallower (17.7 mm against 27 mm), and turns faster at the output (4000 RPM against 1135/1785/2430 RPM). The RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor has more continuous torque (1.45 N·m against 1.3 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.155 N·m/A against 0.087 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (9.4 A against 15 A), and is narrower (85 mm against 88.6 mm).

The RO80 KV105 Lite is US$117 and the RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor is US$149. That is US$90.00 per rated N·m for the RO80 KV105 Lite and US$102.76 per rated N·m for the RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO80 KV105 Lite for more peak torque, less weight, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor for more continuous torque, more torque per amp, a lower current draw and a smaller diameter.
