MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C

H Series. 800 mN·m rated, 2 N·m peak, Ф70 mm, 470 g, 1:1 reduction, 48 V.

US$295.00

800 mN·m rated and 2 N·m peak from a Ф70 x 49.5 mm, 470 g frame, direct drive on 48 V. 2400 RPM rated and 2800 RPM no-load.

Our price is delivered duty paid. Import the same part yourself and you clear customs, pay duty and tariffs, and settle a carrier brokerage fee after the goods have shipped. The cheaper listing is only cheaper if it ships.

A 70 mm direct drive with the bore running straight through it, so the harness passes through the joint rather than around it. Torque comes off the rotor with no gearbox in between: nothing to take up as backlash, and nothing in the drivetrain to wear. The driver, the CAN bus and a 17-bit absolute encoder all sit inside the housing, so this commissions as one part — unlike the bare R-series motors alongside it, which need a driver chosen and tuned before they turn.
