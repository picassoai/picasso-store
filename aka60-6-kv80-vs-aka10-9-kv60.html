AKA60-6 KV80 vs AKA10-9 KV60

The AKA60-6 KV80 draws less current at its rating (4 A against 10.6 A), is lighter (460 g against 1060 g), narrower (80 mm against 100 mm) and shallower (51.2 mm against 70 mm), and turns faster at the output (200/400 RPM against 109 RPM). The AKA10-9 KV60 has more continuous torque (18 N·m against 3 N·m), more peak torque (53 N·m against 9 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.16 N·m/A against 0.11937 N·m/A).

The AKA60-6 KV80 is US$335 and the AKA10-9 KV60 is US$895. That is US$111.67 per rated N·m for the AKA60-6 KV80 and US$49.72 per rated N·m for the AKA10-9 KV60.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKA60-6 KV80 for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AKA10-9 KV60 for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
