AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 vs AK45-10 KV75

The AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 is lighter (190 g against 260 g) and shallower (40.2 mm against 43 mm), and turns faster at the output (370 RPM against 150 RPM). The AK45-10 KV75 has more continuous torque (2.5 N·m against 1.3 N·m), more peak torque (7 N·m against 4.1 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.127 N·m/A against 0.056 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (2.1 A against 2.7 A). They match on diameter.

The AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 is US$152 and the AK45-10 KV75 is US$134. That is US$116.92 per rated N·m for the AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 and US$53.60 per rated N·m for the AK45-10 KV75.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AK40-10 V3.0 KV170 for less weight, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AK45-10 KV75 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
