MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C vs R60 KV115

The MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C has more continuous torque (0.8 N·m against 0.75 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.18 N·m/A against 0.083 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (4.4 A against 9 A). The R60 KV115 has more peak torque (2.3 N·m against 2 N·m), and is lighter (248 g against 470 g) and narrower (69 mm against 70 mm).

The MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C is US$259 and the R60 KV115 is US$200. That is US$323.75 per rated N·m for the MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C and US$266.67 per rated N·m for the R60 KV115.

Which to buy

  • Choose the MyActuator RMD-H-70-15-200-C for more continuous torque, more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
  • Choose the R60 KV115 for more peak torque, less weight and a smaller diameter.
