AKE80-8 KV30 vs AK80-8 KV60 without driver

The AKE80-8 KV30 has more continuous torque (12 N·m against 10 N·m), more peak torque (30 N·m against 25 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.32 N·m/A against 0.199 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (4.8 A against 6.9 A), and is narrower (87 mm against 98 mm) and shallower (32 mm against 43.9 mm). The AK80-8 KV60 without driver turns faster at the output (243 RPM against 150 RPM). They match on weight.

The AKE80-8 KV30 is US$381 and the AK80-8 KV60 without driver is US$526. That is US$31.75 per rated N·m for the AKE80-8 KV30 and US$52.60 per rated N·m for the AK80-8 KV60 without driver.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKE80-8 KV30 for more continuous torque, more peak torque, more torque per amp, a lower current draw, a smaller diameter and less depth.
  • Choose the AK80-8 KV60 without driver for more output speed.
