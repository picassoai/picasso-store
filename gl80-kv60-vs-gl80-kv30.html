GL80 KV60 vs GL80 KV30

The GL80 KV60 turns faster at the output (1010 RPM against 450 RPM). The GL80 KV30 has a higher torque constant (0.356 N·m/A against 0.178 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (2.8 A against 5.6 A). They match on continuous torque, peak torque, weight, diameter and depth.

The GL80 KV60 is US$204 and the GL80 KV30 is US$204. That is US$204.00 per rated N·m for the GL80 KV60 and US$204.00 per rated N·m for the GL80 KV30.

Which to buy

  • Choose the GL80 KV60 for more output speed.
  • Choose the GL80 KV30 for more torque per amp and a lower current draw.
