GL40 KV70 without encoder vs GL40Ⅱ KV82.5

The GL40 KV70 without encoder has a higher torque constant (0.150 N·m/A against 0.11 N·m/A), and draws less current at its rating (1.62 A against 1.88 A), and is lighter (107 g against 112 g) and shallower (21.5 mm against 33.5 mm). The GL40Ⅱ KV82.5 has more peak torque (0.68 N·m against 0.5 N·m), and is narrower (46.1 mm against 46.5 mm), and turns faster at the output (697 RPM against 430 RPM). They match on continuous torque.

The GL40 KV70 without encoder is US$82 and the GL40Ⅱ KV82.5 is US$150. That is US$328.00 per rated N·m for the GL40 KV70 without encoder and US$600.00 per rated N·m for the GL40Ⅱ KV82.5.

Which to buy

  • Choose the GL40 KV70 without encoder for more torque per amp, a lower current draw, less weight and less depth.
  • Choose the GL40Ⅱ KV82.5 for more peak torque, a smaller diameter and more output speed.
