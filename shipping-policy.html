Store/Shipping policy

Shipping policy

Last updated 5 September 2026.

Shipping cost — orders placed on this site

Orders paid online ship to addresses in the United States. Shipping is a flat $50 per order, and is free on orders over $500. It is charged once per order, not per item, so adding a second motor does not add freight.

Import duty and tariffs on US orders

Our goods are manufactured outside the United States, so import duty and tariffs apply to every shipment. On orders to the United States we pay them. The price you see at checkout is what you pay: you will not be billed by the carrier or a customs broker after delivery, and there is nothing to clear at your end.

State sales tax, where it applies, is calculated and shown separately at checkout before you pay.

Lead time

Most orders arrive in about a week. Lead time depends on the model and the quantity, though — some models move quickly, others are supplied against a build schedule — so we confirm it in writing on your order confirmation rather than treating a week as a term of sale, and we tell you before we ship if anything has slipped.

Expedited delivery

Expedited and Saturday delivery can be arranged on request. Because cutoffs vary by destination, expedited orders are arranged individually rather than online — write to info@picassointelligence.com with your address and we will confirm what is possible before you pay.

International orders

We ship internationally, but not through the online checkout — international orders are quoted individually. Import duty, taxes, and customs treatment depend on the parts and on where they ship from and to, and in some destinations the duty is a large fraction of the goods value, so it has to be worked out per country rather than estimated. Where charges apply they are itemised in your quote before you pay. Send us your address and parts list and we will come back with a landed figure.

Oversize and pallet quantities

Pallet quantities ship by freight and are quoted separately. We do not ship batteries or other hazardous goods.

Lost or damaged shipments

Report visible carrier damage within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs of the packaging. We will replace damaged goods and handle the carrier claim ourselves.

Tracking

Tracking is emailed when the label is created, which is usually a few hours before the parcel is collected. If tracking has not moved by the next business day, contact us and we will chase it.
