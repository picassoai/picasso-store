RO80 KV105 Lite vs RI75-PH KV70

The RO80 KV105 Lite has more continuous torque (1.3 N·m against 1.2 N·m) and more peak torque (4.5 N·m against 3.8 N·m), and is lighter (265 g against 348 g) and shallower (17.7 mm against 31.8 mm), and turns faster at the output (4000 RPM against 2365 RPM). The RI75-PH KV70 draws less current at its rating (7.28 A against 15 A), and is narrower (70 mm against 88.6 mm).

The RO80 KV105 Lite is US$117 and the RI75-PH KV70 is US$156. That is US$90.00 per rated N·m for the RO80 KV105 Lite and US$130.00 per rated N·m for the RI75-PH KV70.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO80 KV105 Lite for more continuous torque, more peak torque, less weight, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RI75-PH KV70 for a lower current draw and a smaller diameter.
