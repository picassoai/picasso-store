RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor vs RI75-PH KV70

The RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor has more continuous torque (1.45 N·m against 1.2 N·m) and more peak torque (4.1 N·m against 3.8 N·m), and is shallower (27 mm against 31.8 mm). The RI75-PH KV70 draws less current at its rating (7.28 A against 9.4 A), and is lighter (348 g against 411 g) and narrower (70 mm against 85 mm), and turns faster at the output (2365 RPM against 1135/1785/2430 RPM).

The RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor is US$149 and the RI75-PH KV70 is US$156. That is US$102.76 per rated N·m for the RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor and US$130.00 per rated N·m for the RI75-PH KV70.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RI80 V2.0 KV75 without hall sensor for more continuous torque, more peak torque and less depth.
  • Choose the RI75-PH KV70 for a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter and more output speed.
