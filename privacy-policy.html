What this build actually stores

As shipped, this site has no server and no analytics. The only thing it stores is your cart, held in your own browser's local storage under the key vectra.cart.v1. Clearing site data removes it. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go until a backend is connected.

What we would collect in production

  • Order details: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, and what you bought.
  • Payment confirmation from our processor. Card numbers never reach our servers.
  • Support correspondence you send us.
  • Basic server logs, including IP address, for security and fraud prevention.

What we would not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with the processors needed to fulfil an order — a payment processor, a shipping carrier, and our accounting system.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as tax and warranty obligations require, typically seven years. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion of anything not tied to a legal retention requirement. Write to info@picassointelligence.com.

Cookies

This build sets no cookies. A production storefront typically needs a session cookie for the cart and checkout; any analytics or advertising cookies beyond that should be behind a consent banner.
