Privacy policy

Last updated 5 September 2026.

What we collect

  • Order details. When you check out, Stripe collects your name, email address, phone number, and shipping and billing address, and passes them to us together with what you bought. We use them to fulfil and support the order.
  • Payment details. Card numbers, bank account numbers, and wallet credentials go directly to Stripe and never reach us. We see only the confirmation, the last four digits, and the card brand.
  • What you send us. Messages sent through the contact form, or by email, are delivered to us through Formspree and kept as correspondence.

How your order is fulfilled

Orders ship directly from our manufacturing partner rather than from a warehouse we operate. To make that happen we pass your name, delivery address, phone number, and the contents of your order to that partner and to the carrier, both of which are outside the United States. That is the only purpose the information is used for, and it is not used for marketing by anyone.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. This site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no third-party trackers. Information is shared only with the parties needed to complete an order — our payment processor, our manufacturing and shipping partners, the carrier, and our accountant — and with government agencies where customs or export rules require it.

Cookies and browser storage

We set no advertising or analytics cookies. Your cart is held in your own browser's local storage and never leaves your device until you check out; clearing site data removes it. Stripe sets its own cookies on the checkout page it hosts, which are necessary for payment and fraud prevention.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as tax, customs, export, and warranty obligations require — typically seven years. Correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful for supporting what you bought.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything not tied to a legal retention requirement. Write to info@picassointelligence.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in California, the EU, or the UK, you have these rights by statute and we apply them to everyone.

Contact

Picasso Intelligence LLC — info@picassointelligence.com. Questions about this policy, or about a specific order, reach a person at that address.
