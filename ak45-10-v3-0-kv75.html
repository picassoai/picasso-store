AK45-10 V3.0 KV75

AK Series. 2.5 N·m rated, 7 N·m peak, Ф53 mm, 262 g, 10:1 reduction, 24 V.

US$175.00

2.5 N·m rated and 7 N·m peak from a Ф53 x 45.2 mm, 262 g frame, geared 10:1, on 24 V. Kv 75, 120 RPM rated and 180 RPM no-load. CubeMars lists it for legged robots, exoskeletons and AGVs.

The V3.0 revision of the AK45-10, on the current driver board: one connector carrying power and CAN instead of two, and the V3.0 tool for calibration and CAN ID. Dual encoders, on the rotor and the output, so the joint reports where the load actually is rather than where the motor thinks it put it.
