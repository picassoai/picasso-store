Neck joint actuators

Two or three degrees of freedom carrying only the head and its sensors — the lightest joint in the machine. Torque is rarely the deciding factor; smooth low-speed motion is. Head motion is what people read as lifelike, and any cogging or velocity ripple also shows up directly in whatever the head-mounted cameras are recording.
