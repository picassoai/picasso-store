AKE60-8 KV80 vs AKE80-8 KV30

The AKE60-8 KV80 is lighter (260 g against 570 g), narrower (69 mm against 87 mm) and shallower (25 mm against 32 mm), and turns faster at the output (180 RPM against 150 RPM). The AKE80-8 KV30 has more continuous torque (12 N·m against 5 N·m), more peak torque (30 N·m against 12.5 N·m) and a higher torque constant (0.32 N·m/A against 0.13 N·m/A). They match on current draw.

The AKE60-8 KV80 is US$245 and the AKE80-8 KV30 is US$381. That is US$49.00 per rated N·m for the AKE60-8 KV80 and US$31.75 per rated N·m for the AKE80-8 KV30.

Which to buy

  • Choose the AKE60-8 KV80 for less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the AKE80-8 KV30 for more continuous torque, more peak torque and more torque per amp.
