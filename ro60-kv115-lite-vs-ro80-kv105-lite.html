RO60 KV115 Lite vs RO80 KV105 Lite

The RO60 KV115 Lite has a higher torque constant (0.094 N·m/A against 0.087 N·m/A), draws less current at its rating (8.5 A against 15 A), is lighter (195 g against 265 g), narrower (70.5 mm against 88.6 mm) and shallower (15 mm against 17.7 mm), and turns faster at the output (4200 RPM against 4000 RPM). The RO80 KV105 Lite has more continuous torque (1.3 N·m against 0.8 N·m) and more peak torque (4.5 N·m against 2.4 N·m).

The RO60 KV115 Lite is US$87 and the RO80 KV105 Lite is US$117. That is US$108.75 per rated N·m for the RO60 KV115 Lite and US$90.00 per rated N·m for the RO80 KV105 Lite.

Which to buy

  • Choose the RO60 KV115 Lite for more torque per amp, a lower current draw, less weight, a smaller diameter, less depth and more output speed.
  • Choose the RO80 KV105 Lite for more continuous torque and more peak torque.
