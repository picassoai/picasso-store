AK40-10 KV170

AK Series. 1.3 N·m rated, 4.1 N·m peak, Ф53 mm, 200 g, 10:1 reduction, 24 V.

The smallest joint in the AK line: brushless motor, planetary reducer, encoder and driver in one housing. Sized for the places an actuator has to disappear into the limb — humanoid wrists, grippers, small pan-tilt heads. Supplied without the driver board.
