SW12 Thruster CW vs DW10 Thruster CW

The SW12 Thruster CW puts out more thrust (≥12 kgf against ≥10 kgf). The DW10 Thruster CW is rated deeper (0–350 m against 0–30 m) and lighter in air (845 g against 1180 g).

The SW12 Thruster CW is US$381 and the DW10 Thruster CW is US$336. That is US$31.75 per kgf of thrust for the SW12 Thruster CW and US$33.60 per kgf of thrust for the DW10 Thruster CW.

Which to buy

  • Choose the SW12 Thruster CW for more thrust.
  • Choose the DW10 Thruster CW for a greater working depth and less weight in air.
